How calendar events are created

🤖Automatic: Every website booking instantly creates a Google Calendar event with client name, address, service type, and phone number.
🌟First-time clients show in gold (banana color) so you can give extra attention to new customers.
📩Client receives a .ics calendar file in their confirmation email — Gmail and Apple Mail show an "Add to Calendar" button automatically.
🔔Reminders: Email 24h before, email 2h before, popup 30 min before — all set automatically on every booking.
🎨Color coding: Blue = cleaning, Orange = power wash, Purple = commercial, Gold = new client, Green = US holidays.
📋Invoice: Send an invoice anytime via POST /api/cleaning/invoice — auto-branded email sent to client.
