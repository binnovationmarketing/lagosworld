Frequently Asked Questions

Lagos Cleaning Services & CH Elite Washing

Booking & Pricing

How do I book a service?

Request a service on our website, call/text us, or message us on WhatsApp. We'll confirm availability and send you a date and arrival window.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the service, the size and condition of the space, and frequency. Standard residential cleaning and power washing are quoted up front — for larger or commercial jobs we may request a photo or quick assessment. Contact us for a free quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash and Zelle (US), and Pix or bank transfer for clients in Brazil. Payment is generally due on completion unless otherwise arranged.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes — first-time customers get 15% off, plus seasonal deals and referral credits. Offers can't be combined unless stated.

Service & Coverage

What areas do you serve?

Philadelphia, PA and surrounding areas across Pennsylvania and New Jersey. Outside our standard area? Contact us — travel surcharges may apply.

What cleaning services do you offer?

House & apartment cleaning, move-in/move-out, one-time deep cleans, recurring service, and office/commercial cleaning. Power washing is handled by our CH Elite Washing division.

What does CH Elite Washing clean?

Driveways and concrete, decks and patios, house siding (soft wash), roofs (low-pressure soft wash), gutters, and commercial exteriors. Results vary by surface and pre-existing condition — we'll set expectations before we start.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily — many clients provide access (key, code, or unlocked gate). Just make sure our team can get in, and secure pets. See our Service & Scheduling Policy.

Trust & Guarantees

Are you insured?

Yes. Our crews are insured, uniformed, and background-checked, and we use eco-friendly, surface-safe products.

What if I'm not satisfied?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll return to re-address the areas of concern at no extra charge. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Contact us as early as possible — we don't charge cancellation fees. You can cancel or reschedule free of charge anytime. Details in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
