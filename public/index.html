The Founder

Dayane LagoA Story of Victory

She was born in southern Brazil and raised in Jaru, Rondônia — a small city that gave her something no city can manufacture: the foundation of a strong family, the courage to dream, and the faith to follow.

She married young. And with her heart full of hope and her bags packed with everything she had, Dayane Lago crossed the ocean to build a life in America — not because it was easy, but because she believed it was possible.

"I didn't come here looking for a shortcut. I came here looking for a future — for myself, and one day, for every woman who would walk the same path."

That was over 20 years ago. Two decades of learning, building, failing, rising. Two decades of pouring herself into homes and businesses across Pennsylvania and New Jersey — until her cleaning business became a name people trusted, recommended, and called first.

Lagos World is the fruit of that journey. A platform born from sweat, faith, and an unbreakable belief: that every woman deserves beauty, dignity, and the opportunity to rise.

— Dayane Lago, Founder · Lagos World
