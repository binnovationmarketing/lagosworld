SummaryWe collect the information needed to process and ship your jewelry orders, respond to you (including via our Milla chat assistant), and — with your consent — send offers. We never sell your data, and you can opt out of marketing anytime.

1. Who We Are

Lagos Jewelry ("we," "us," "our") is the retail jewelry brand of Lagos World LLC, based in Philadelphia, PA, selling 18k gold-plated semi-jewelry online at lagosworld.app. Contact: admin.lagosworld@gmail.com · +1 (215) 626-2345.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 You provide

  • Orders/checkout: name, email, phone, shipping address (street, city, state, ZIP), items ordered, and payment method selected (Cash, Zelle, or Pix). We do not store full card numbers — payments are made directly via Cash/Zelle/Pix and you upload a proof of payment where applicable.
  • Newsletter: name and email.
  • Chat (Milla): messages you send to our AI assistant and any contact details you provide there.

2.2 Collected automatically

  • Usage/analytics data (pages, device, referrer), cookies and advertising pixels, and IP address.

3. How We Use It

  • Process, fulfill, and ship your orders, and provide customer support.
  • Send order confirmations, shipping updates, and payment-proof requests (transactional).
  • Send promotions, new arrivals, and offers if you opted in (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • Operate the Milla chat assistant to recommend products and answer questions.
  • Measure and run advertising (Google, Meta) and improve the store.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that run our store — Supabase (database), Vercel (hosting), Gmail/Nodemailer (email), Groq/Google (the Milla AI assistant), Google & Meta (analytics/ads) — and with delivery partners to ship your order, or where required by law.

5. Cookies & Ads

We use essential, analytics, and advertising cookies. Manage them in your browser, or opt out of ad personalization at Google and Meta.

6. Retention

Order records are kept up to 3 years for business/tax purposes; marketing data until you unsubscribe; analytics per provider defaults.

7. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data, and opt out of marketing. California residents: see our California Notice at Collection. To exercise rights, email admin.lagosworld@gmail.com.

8. Security & Children

We use HTTPS/TLS, access controls, and reputable infrastructure to protect your data, though no method is 100% secure. Our store is not directed to children under 13.
