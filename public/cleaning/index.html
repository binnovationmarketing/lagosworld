FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cleaning services.

How much does house cleaning cost in Philadelphia? +

Our pricing depends on the size of your home and the type of cleaning. A typical 2-bedroom apartment starts around $120–$160. A 3–4 bedroom house ranges from $160–$240. We provide exact flat-rate quotes before any work begins — no hidden fees, ever. Call us or fill out our booking form for a free personalized quote.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning? +

No, you don't need to be home. Many of our clients provide entry instructions (lockbox, key, or code) and we take care of everything. All our cleaners are background-checked, bonded, and insured for your peace of mind. You'll receive a completion notification when we're done.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment? +

Yes! We bring all necessary cleaning products and equipment. We use professional-grade, eco-friendly cleaning products that are safe for your family and pets. If you have a preference for specific products or have allergies, just let us know and we'll accommodate you.

What areas do you serve in PA and NJ? +

We serve the greater Philadelphia metro area, including all of Philadelphia, Montgomery County, Delaware County, and Bucks County in Pennsylvania. In New Jersey, we cover Camden County, Burlington County, and surrounding areas including Cherry Hill, Voorhees, Mount Laurel, and more. Not sure if we cover your area? Call us and we'll check!

What is your satisfaction guarantee? +

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with any aspect of your cleaning, contact us within 24 hours and we will return to re-clean any missed areas at no additional charge. Your satisfaction is our priority and we stand behind every job we do.

How far in advance do I need to book? +

We recommend booking 48–72 hours in advance for regular cleanings. For move-in/move-out cleanings or power washing, 3–5 days is ideal. However, we do offer same-day and next-day availability depending on our schedule. Call us for urgent bookings — we do our best to accommodate last-minute requests.

Are your cleaners background-checked and insured? +

Absolutely. Every Lagos Cleaning team member undergoes a thorough background check before they are hired. We are fully bonded and insured, which means you are protected in the unlikely event of any damage or incident. We take the trust you place in us very seriously.

What is the CH ELITE Power Wash service? +

CH ELITE is our premium pressure washing service using commercial-grade equipment and biodegradable detergents. It's designed for deep cleaning of exterior surfaces including driveways, decks, patios, porches, and siding. The "Elite" designation means we use specialized techniques and take extra care to protect your surfaces while delivering maximum cleaning power. We provide a before-and-after photo report with every job.

How does your referral program work? +

When you refer a friend or family member to Lagos Cleaning and they complete their first cleaning, you receive a $25 credit applied to your next service — automatically. Your referred friend also receives 10% off their first booking. There's no limit to how many people you can refer, so the savings add up quickly. Just give them your name when they book.
