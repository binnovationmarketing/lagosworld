Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to an accessible experience for everyone

Effective DateJune 1, 2026 Last UpdatedJune 1, 2026 StandardWCAG 2.1 Level AA (target)

1. Our Commitment

Lagos Cleaning Services and CH Elite Washing are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

2. Conformance Status

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities. Accessibility is an ongoing effort and we work to meet or exceed these standards across our site.

3. Measures We Take

  • Semantic HTML structure and descriptive headings
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Sufficient color contrast for text and interactive elements
  • Keyboard-navigable menus, forms, and controls
  • Responsive layouts that adapt to mobile and assistive technologies
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences in animations

4. Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some content (such as third-party embeds, maps, or chat widgets) may not be fully accessible. We are working to address these areas and welcome your feedback.

5. Feedback

If you encounter any accessibility barrier on our website, or need information in an alternative format, please contact us. We treat accessibility requests as a priority and aim to respond within 5 business days.
